## Brand & Style

This design system blends **Tactile Skeuomorphism** with **Playful Gamification** and crisp modern mobile ergonomics. Crafted for dual personas—young children mastering early habits and busy parents orchestrating daily routines—the aesthetic balances exuberant childlike wonder with calm parental clarity.

### Emotional Resonance
- **For the Child:** Feelings of achievement, tactile delight, security, and game-like celebration. Every interaction provides instant physical satisfaction, reminiscent of physical toy buttons, popping stickers, and collecting sparkling tokens into an actual jar.
- **For the Parent:** Clarity, reassurance, effortless speed, and control without friction. The parent interface strips away high-stimulation noise in favor of structured cards, clean status badges, and rapid one-tap approvals.

### Design Principles
1. **Chunky & Touch-First:** Oversized interactive components with minimum 56px touch targets, generous padding, and pillowy affordances tailored for developing motor skills.
2. **Physicality & Tactility:** Solid 3D bevels, distinct bottom edge offsets ("press-down" depths), and elastic state transitions emulate physical pushbuttons rather than flat glass panes.
3. **Warm Atmospheric Light:** Soft warm whites and cream surfaces prevent screen fatigue and evoke the pages of a beloved storybook.

## Colors

The palette relies on vibrant, sun-drenched primaries anchored by organic neutrals. 

- **Primary (`#F59E0B` Amber / `#FBBF24` Sunny Gold):** The celebratory token core. Represents collected stars, active quest highlights, and congratulatory states. Paired with `#B45309` for its physical bottom-edge button shelf.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7` Ocean / `#38BDF8` Sky):** The structural guide. Powers parent navigation badges, progress rings, child quest headers, and peaceful nighttime indicators. Paired with `#0369A1` for push-depth.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` Fresh Mint):** The completion signal. Used for instant "Done" affirmations, parent approval checkmarks, and streak milestones.
- **Accent Coral (`#FB923C`):** Highlights high-priority quests, reward redemption calls-to-action, and urgent pending items.
- **Canvas & Surface Neutrals:** Base backgrounds utilize soft `#F8FAFC` tinted with warm storybook warmth (`#FEF9C3` for star jars and earned cards). Text avoids absolute black, relying on `#0F172A` for high-contrast titles and `#475569` for friendly, readable instructional microcopy.

## Typography

Typography bridges readability for emerging readers with functional ergonomics for parents:

- **Headline & Label Font (Quicksand):** Selected for its geometric rounded terminals, cheerful openness, and organic, non-threatening silhouettes. Used for all headers, star counters, chip indicators, and interactive CTAs.
- **Body Font (Nunito Sans):** Complements Quicksand with sturdy, highly legible letterforms at micro sizes. Provides optical clarity for parents reviewing quest conditions and children reading short prompts.
- **Weight Strategy:** Minimum weight is set to `500` across all contexts; thin weights are strictly avoided to ensure accessibility in bright sunlight and on budget tablet displays.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Star scores and counters leverage `number-counter` (`Quicksand` Bold) with tabular figures for steady animation while stars tumble into the jar.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is touch-optimized, content-focused, and portrait-first:

- **Touch Safety:** Spacing ensures a minimum safe clearance of `space-md` (20px) between competing tap targets to eliminate accidental gestures by children.
- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Mobile (<640px):** Single-column dynamic stream with `margin` of 16px and 12px item gaps. Focuses visual attention purely on one quest card at a time.
  - **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 2-column or 3-column masonry grid for rewards and quest queues, utilizing a 24px gutter and 32px canvas margins.
  - **Desktop / Large Dashboard (>1024px):** Centered phone/tablet viewport preview (max-width 560px for child preview) alongside a 2-column parent management panel.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous vertical component pads (`space-md` to `space-lg`) provide clear boundaries between quest blocks, celebratory modals, and bottom navigation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical, game-inspired depth rather than misty atmospheric shadows:

### The "3D Push" System
Interactive components utilize a dual-layer construct:
1. **The Face:** The top rounded surface containing label and icon.
2. **The Base / Shelf:** A darker, saturated lip beneath the element (3px to 5px solid offset) that creates a physical button feel.
3. **Active Depressed State:** On tap or press, the face shifts downward `transform: translateY(4px)`, collapsing the visible shelf and delivering instantaneous haptic and mechanical visual feedback.

### Elevation Layers
- **Surface Level 0 (Ground):** Pure `#F8FAFC` or warm cream canvas with soft decorative background blobs.
- **Surface Level 1 (Cards & Containers):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` or `#FEF9C3` cards with a crisp `2px solid #E2E8F0` border and a soft ambient drop shadow (`0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`).
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Floating Action & Star Jar):** Heavy tactile float using dual shadows: a solid 4px tactile shelf plus `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)` to create a glowing halo.
- **Surface Level 3 (PIN Pad & Modals):** High-focus sheets overlaid on a 60% frosted slate scrim (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is hyper-rounded, soft, and organic:

- **Border Radius Philosophy:** Follows `Pill-shaped` (Tier 3). Sharp 90-degree corners are forbidden.
- **Cards & Quest Tiles:** Use heavy `rounded-3xl` (24px to 32px) radii to look friendly, chewable, and soft.
- **Buttons & Chips:** Fully rounded pills (`rounded-full` / 9999px) or ultra-chunky rounded rectangles (`rounded-2xl` with minimum 20px curves).
- **Avatars & Star Jars:** Circular badges with thick protective borders (3px to 4px white stroke) to simulate die-cut vinyl stickers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Chunky Button ("Selesai" / "Tukar Hadiah"):** Minimum height 56px (64px in Child Mode). Pill-shaped or `rounded-2xl`. Features a 4px solid bottom offset border (`tactile-shadow-*`). Active state drops down by 4px with zero transition time for immediate physical click responsiveness.
- **Secondary Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `3px solid #E2E8F0` border, `Quicksand` Bold label in `#475569`. Pressed state shows a `#F1F5F9` tint.
- **Icon-Only Touch Buttons:** 56x56px circular push buttons with centered glyphs, designed for audio triggers and fast back-navigation.

### Quest Cards
- Structured container with white background, 24px rounded corners, and a 2px outer border in `#F1F5F9`.
- **Left Slot:** 56x56px colorful badge with a high-contrast quest icon (toothbrush, book, toys, bed).
- **Center Content:** Quest title (`headline-sm`), category tag, and the star incentive badge (`+2 ⭐`).
- **Right Action Slot:** A large pushable "Done" button or an animated "Menunggu" (Pending) clock status chip.

### Star Bank / Celengan Bintang
- A rounded glassmorphic/translucent amphora jar with a warm golden liquid filling or stacked star sprites.
- Star balance displayed via `number-counter` with a floating golden star badge that pulses gently when new approvals drop in.

### Parent PIN Modal & Gate
- Screen security gate featuring 4 pillowy circular passcode indicator nodes.
- High-contrast, large keypad (keys 68x68px) with satisfying press mechanics and clear parent-facing label cues.

### Reward Catalog Cards
- Vertical layout with a photographic or illustrated card banner, pill-shaped star cost badge on the top right (`5 ⭐`), item title, and a disabled state when current stars < cost.
- Active states feature a gentle golden border pulse (`#FBBF24`).

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes (Parent Mode)
- **Inputs:** 52px height, 16px corner radius, inset soft shadow, clear placeholder copy in Nunito Sans.
- **Checkboxes & Toggles:** Chunky slider switches with smooth 32px thumb pills that turn from neutral gray to vibrant mint green on toggle.
